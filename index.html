Correcting misleading wholesale prices in Shopify

Diagnosing misleading collection-card prices and choosing a product-configuration change suited to the team’s sample-order workflow.

What I implemented

The shop overview displayed the intended price, and a free sample draft order worked as expected in my checks.

I traced the issue to a hidden sample variant and implemented a configuration workaround with ordering guidance for Sales, avoiding custom theme development.

View the wholesale page I built
Read the diagnosis, decision, and delivery

The customer-facing issue

Collection cards showed unusually low prices for wholesale accounts in a Shopify shop using BSS wholesale functionality.

How I investigated

  1. Replicated relevant customer account tags on a test profile.
  2. Reproduced the pricing display under the affected account conditions.
  3. Inspected the theme’s Liquid pricing logic and product variants.
  4. Traced the displayed minimum to a hidden 100g sample variant included in product.price_min.

The decision I made

I initially proposed a Liquid adjustment to exclude sample variants from the displayed minimum. After considering how samples were used, I chose a configuration workaround: samples were mainly used in draft orders and internal café orders, and were frequently provided free of charge.

Option consideredCustom Liquid change

Filter sample variants out of the displayed minimum-price calculation.

Selected approachConfiguration + ordering guidance

Match the sample’s listed price to the 250g bag and apply a 100% discount when the sample should be free in a draft order.

My implementation and the trade-off

I changed the sample’s price and communicated the discount instruction internally. This avoided commissioning a custom theme change for the selected solution.

The trade-off was operational: staff needed to apply the discount when preparing a free sample order. The instruction was part of the workaround.

How I checked the workaround

I checked the layout and pricing display on the shop overview page and created a sample draft order as an example for the Sales team. Both checks worked as intended.

Related page build

I coordinated, designed, and built the wholesale page, including its layout structure, formatting, and text alignment. I worked with the team on German translations. The linked live page reflects this work.

The linked page demonstrates my page implementation work. The pricing investigation concerned collection cards for wholesale accounts.

Further Shopify operations

  • Reviewed Google Analytics and Search Console data before removing outdated, untranslated location pages from public view.
  • Updated German product descriptions and accessory pages, comparing live content against the English source.
  • Worked with theme support to troubleshoot and test mobile alignment, padding, slideshows, and buttons.
  • Investigated a tax-display discrepancy in customer orders and restored a missing product tax tag.

Resolving a checkout rounding discrepancy

I defined and prioritized launch testing from the product page through checkout. The checks exposed a one-cent difference associated with product and checkout rounding in our WooCommerce setup.

Implemented & checked

I adjusted decimal precision, tested the change in staging, and implemented the confirmed solution in production. I then checked it against different countries’ tax rates in English and Spanish.

I reported the issue to developers for consideration of a longer-term improvement.

Read the investigation and test criteria

My investigation and validation

  1. Set testing criteria and prioritized launch requirements by risk.
  2. Identified a one-cent checkout discrepancy linked to differences in rounding within the shop setup.
  3. Used AI to help work through the calculation and identify a proposed solution.
  4. Updated decimal precision, validated the change in staging, and applied the confirmed solution in production.
  5. Rechecked using different countries’ tax rates, in English and Spanish, and reported the issue to developers.

Related check: Finance corrected a price list

In a separate promotional price-list check, I found that a listed final price did not reconcile with its underlying calculation. I compared the figures against the live shop with the applicable coupons and reported the inconsistency. Finance corrected the price list.

This was a separate price-list discrepancy, rather than the one-cent checkout issue described above. Internal prices, formulas, and market-specific pricing rules are omitted.

Preventing repeat issues

I followed up with Finance with proposed prevention guidance covering calculation precision, reconciliation of bundle line items, coupon checks, checkout validation, shared pricing references, and exception logging.

The guidance was submitted to Finance for validation. Its approval and wider adoption are not confirmed.
